# Reference — English

Goal: sound like a native speaker wrote it, not an AI. Grammar is rarely the problem in AI English — the *patterns* are.

## Register & mechanics

- **Use contractions** (it's, you'll, we're) unless the register is formal/legal.
- **Match spelling to the audience.** US (color, organize) vs UK/international (colour, organise). Pick one and stay consistent. For an EU/international audience, UK or international English reads well.
- **Second person is fine.** "you" throughout; warm and direct for product/marketing.

## AI-tell phrases (cut or replace)

Openers/fillers: "In today's fast-paced world…", "In an increasingly digital age…", "It's important to note that…", "It's worth mentioning that…", "When it comes to…", "Whether you're X or Y…", "Let's dive in / delve into…", "At the end of the day…", "In conclusion / In summary".

Jargon/superlatives: "seamless", "robust", "powerful and intuitive", "leverage", "unlock the potential", "elevate", "revolutionize", "game-changer", "cutting-edge", "empower", "streamline", "supercharge", "boasts", "navigate the complexities", "a testament to", "in the realm of", "tapestry".

## Structure tells

Tricolons everywhere ("fast, easy, and efficient"), "not only… but also…", perfectly balanced parallel sentences, em-dash every other sentence, everything in bullets, bold on every third word, an emoji per line, "Firstly/Secondly/Finally". Break the pattern; return to prose with rhythm.

## Sounding human

Vary sentence length — a short punchy sentence after a long one lands. Active voice, concrete verbs, plain words over Latinate ones ("use" not "utilize", "help" not "facilitate"). Cut hedging ("essentially", "basically", "arguably") unless it earns its place.

## Examples

**1 —** Before: "In today's fast-paced world, our powerful and intuitive app lets you seamlessly access your portfolio in the blink of an eye."
After: "Your whole portfolio in one place. Connect your accounts and see everything in seconds."

**2 —** Before: "It's important to note that our robust solution empowers teams to streamline their workflows and unlock new levels of productivity."
After: "The app helps teams cut the busywork — fewer manual steps, fewer mistakes."

**3 —** Before: "Whether you're a beginner or a pro, this game-changing tool will revolutionize the way you invest."
After: "New to investing or been at it for years — you'll find your way around fast."
